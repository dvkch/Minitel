Minitel 1B stickers — 1 / 2: colors

Print this page on the sticker vinyl (colors depend on the paper), let it dry, then compare in daylight by laying the swatches on the keys.
Copy the hex of the best match into the colors at the top of stickers-variables.css and reload. The bold value is the current one, and the grids re-center on it,
so a second round with smaller steps (in stickers-swatches.js) gets you even closer. Then print page 2 on the vinyl.

This line must measure exactly 10 cm. Print with margins "None", scale 100 % and background graphics on.
